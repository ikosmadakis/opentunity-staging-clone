The OPENTUNITY project aims to accelerate the digitalization of the energy sector in line with the EU’s Energy Digitalization Roadmap. It will deploy innovative tools that support grid operators and empower prosumers to unlock energy flexibility. These solutions will be tested in large-scale pilots across Greece, Slovenia, Spain, and Switzerland, promoting renewable energy integration, e-mobility, and advanced flexibility markets. Ultimately, the project seeks to reduce system costs and enhance electricity market efficiency.

Blue Sun Automation Ltd leads Task 3.3 of the OPENTUNITY project, which focuses on developing services for the plug-and-play recognition, registration, and integration of end-user devices into the OPENTUNITY ecosystem. This task enables the extraction of key data—such as nominal characteristics and operational constraints—essential for unlocking energy flexibility. The aggregated data will be made accessible to various stakeholders, including Home or Building Energy Management Systems, to support the efficient use of energy flexibility. Blue Sun is also exploring how this methodology can be adapted for legacy equipment, promoting broader adoption by industrial players and market participants both within and beyond the OPENTUNITY ecosystem.
